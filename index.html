What we do

End-to-end, under one roof

Design and execution handled together, so the idea you approve is the space you get.

01

Architectural Design & Planning

From site study to sanction-ready drawings — floor plans, elevations and 3D concepts that turn a plot into a buildable, livable design.

02

Interior Design & Execution

Turnkey interiors — space planning, bespoke joinery, materials, lighting and styling, executed and finished on site by our own team.

03

Facade & Elevation Design

Facades that give your building a striking, durable public face — engineered and built end to end, not just drawn.

04

3D Miniature Model Making

Physical scale models you can hold and walk around — so you experience the project before a single brick is laid.

We're a Kolkata-based team of architects, designers and builders who care as much about how a space is made as how it looks. Our approach is rooted in authenticity, thoughtful detail, and real respect for each client's vision.
